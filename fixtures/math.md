# Math

Inline math sits on the text baseline: $E = mc^2$, a fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, and a sum
$\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$.

Display math gets its own centred line:

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

A matrix and a radical:

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}
$$

## Energy $E = mc^2$

| Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ | Pythagoras |
| $\sqrt{2}$ | Irrational |

Without the engine, every formula falls back to its source.

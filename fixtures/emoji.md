# Emoji presentation

Text-default characters with the emoji selector (U+FE0F) must be in colour:
❤️ ⚠️ ✔️ ☀️ ✨ ☎️ 1️⃣ 🏳️‍🌈 👩‍💻 👍🏽

Always in colour: 🚀 🎉 👍 😀

Shortcodes: :heart: :warning: :sparkles: :rocket:

Without the selector these stay text glyphs in the text font: ✔ → © ™ ☀ ❤

In `code ❤️ ⚠️` and **bold ❤️ ⚠️** and *italic 1️⃣ 👍🏽* and a [link ❤️](https://example.com).

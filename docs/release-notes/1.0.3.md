mdview 1.0.3: an offer to close tabs you have not opened in a week.

## New

- **Close tabs not opened in a week.** At start, a bar offers to close the tabs you have not opened in the last 7 days you used mdview (days you do not use it are not counted). Close them, Keep (their count starts again), or close the bar to be asked at the next start. Pinned tabs and the tab showing are never offered. On by default: Settings, "Offer to close tabs not opened in a week".

## Changed

- **Settings** fits in the window again on Windows: the tab settings are now in the group "Content and tabs", and fewer rows have a help line.

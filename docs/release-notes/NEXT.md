## New

- **Menus fade in.** The main menu, the context menus and the Open recent submenu fade in and settle into place as they open, quickly; they close at once.

- **An "Animations" setting.** On the Appearance page of Settings, on unless turned off: off, menus open at once with no fade. When the system asks for less motion (Windows: Settings, Accessibility, Visual effects, "Animation effects" off; macOS: "Reduce motion"), menus open at once whatever this setting says. "Play animated images" under Reading is a separate setting, for animated GIF and WebP pictures only, and is unchanged.

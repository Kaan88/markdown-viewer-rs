mdview 1.0.2: pinned tabs, and a setting to start on the last pinned tab.

## New

- **Pinned tabs.** Right-click a tab and choose Pin tab (also in the command palette). Pinned tabs stay first, show a pin where the close button was (click it to unpin), are not closed by Ctrl+W, a middle-click, Close other tabs or Close all tabs, and come back pinned at the next start.
- **Start on the last pinned tab** (Settings, Content): the window opens on the pinned tab you used last instead of the tab you left showing. Off by default.

## Fixed

- **The tab showing when the window closed** is the one that shows at the next start, kept by its file rather than by its position.

mdview 1.2.2: text boxes that take the whole mouse, and drags that end when the window loses the focus.

This release moves mdview onto the latest shared toolkit (`markdown-shared-rs` `84c0fc8`), whose
text boxes now handle the mouse as a whole.

## Text boxes

- **The command palette's box and the box of the list of slides** take a double click (a word), a
  triple click (all of it), Shift and a click to extend, and a drag past either end, which
  scrolls the text on. A right click or Shift+F10 opens their edit menu: Cut, Copy, Paste, Select
  all.
- **The find box** scrolls its text on when a selection is dragged past either end, and takes a
  click in its border or on the magnifier at the nearest end of the text.

## Fixes

- **Switching away during a drag** (Alt+Tab, or a dialog opening) no longer leaves the drag held:
  the next move after coming back no longer drags. A pane keeps the width it was dragged to, a
  press on the page or a slide follows no link, and a button, row or category held down is not
  clicked. This covers the page, the panes, the find box, the command palette, the list of slides
  and Settings.

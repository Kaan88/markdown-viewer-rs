mdview 1.0.5 (not released yet): Settings as pages.

## Changed

- **Settings** shows one page at a time, as mdedit's: Appearance, Reading, Tabs and Updates in a list at the left, nothing that scrolls, at any window size. Every setting is where its page says and keeps its value. "Looked at for less than" is shown under its switch and is greyed out, with the reason, while the switch is off. The dialog opens again on the page you last looked at.

## Fixed

- **Closing the previewed tab tells mdedit.** When a tab mdedit's "Preview in mdview" had opened was closed here, mdedit kept "Preview in mdview" checked and said "Previewing" as if nothing had happened. mdview now tells it (with mdedit 1.3.0 or later), and mdedit ends that preview; closing mdview's window tells it the same for every such tab.
